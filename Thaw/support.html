Thaw — Support

Need help with Thaw, the app that gives you something to say when a conversation stalls? We’re happy to help.

Contact us

Email us with any questions, bug reports, or feature requests:
contact.splatterflow@gmail.com
We typically reply within 1–2 business days. The fastest way is Settings → Contact Support in the app, which fills in your app version and device for us.

Common questions

  • What do I get each time? A question to start with, two or three more to ask once they answer, and a line to change the subject if it goes nowhere. It’s a whole conversation, not one question.
  • What do “That worked” and “Didn’t land” do? They set how personal the next question is. Level 1 is about where you are; level 5 is about what matters to them. “That worked” goes up a level, “Didn’t land” goes down one, and Thaw only ever moves one level at a time, so a stranger never gets the deep ones.
  • Why am I seeing the same one again? Thaw remembers the last 50 it showed you and skips them. The free version has 42 conversations, so after a few uses some will come back. Pro has 207.
  • What’s in Pro? All 207 conversations, every situation, every level. One payment, no subscription. The free version already covers every situation, so you never pay just to make the app work.
  • How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore Purchases.” Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. No account in the app is needed.
  • Does Thaw need the internet? No. Everything is already in the app, and nothing you do is sent anywhere.
  • How do achievements work? They mark real things that happened: conversations that worked, how deep you got, the situations you’ve used. No streaks, nothing to keep up. They’re saved on this phone only.
