Privacy Policy

For the Thaw iOS app · Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Splatterflow (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Thaw mobile application (the “App”). Thaw is built to need nothing from you: it collects no personal information, requires no account, and never sells or shares data.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect personal data (no names, emails, contacts, or location).
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind.
  • The App does not require an account, login, or registration.
  • All conversation content ships inside the App. Nothing you do in the App is sent anywhere.
  • Your settings, your language choice, your Pro purchase status, and your achievements are stored locally on your device.

2. What the App stores on your device

Thaw keeps a small amount of information in the App’s own storage on your device so it can work the way you left it: the language and appearance you chose, whether you have seen the introduction, which conversation threads you have recently been shown (so they are not repeated), your achievement progress, and whether you have purchased Thaw Pro. None of this leaves your device, and deleting the App deletes all of it.

3. Network use

The App works fully offline. The only network request it makes is to fetch a small public list of other Splatterflow apps, shown in Settings under “More from Splatterflow”. That request carries no information about you, and the list is cached on your device. Opening one of those apps shows Apple’s own App Store page inside the App; Apple’s privacy policy applies to that page.

4. Purchases

Thaw Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The App only records on your device that the purchase was made, so it can unlock the full content. You can restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
