Need help with Slow Drip, the app that shows how much caffeine is still in your body and when you can sleep? We’re happy to help.

Common questions

  • How is “in your body now” calculated? Each drink is absorbed over about 45 minutes, then half of it leaves every 5 hours. Slow Drip adds your drinks together. It is an estimate based on average metabolism, not a measurement and not medical advice.
  • I just logged a coffee and the number barely moved. Why? A drink takes about 45 minutes to be absorbed, so the number climbs gradually. The drop next to it shows how much has been absorbed so far; the curve and the sleep time update at once.
  • Caffeine affects me more (or less) than that. Change it under Settings → Caffeine sensitivity: High (7 hours), Normal (5) or Low (3.5).
  • I logged a drink late. Can I fix the time? Yes. On Today, tap “logged today”, then tap the time next to the drink.
  • What’s in Pro? Unlimited “will this keep me up?” checks, the last-call reminder, more caffeine sources, History, widgets, the Live Activity and Apple Health sync. One payment, no subscription. Logging and the curve are free.
  • How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore Purchases.” Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. No account in the app is needed.
  • How does Apple Health work? It is off until you switch it on in Settings. Slow Drip then saves the caffeine of each drink to Health and counts caffeine that other apps saved there. Switching it off removes the imported entries from Slow Drip.
  • The widget shows “Unlock”. Widgets are part of Pro. If you have Pro, open the app once so the widget can update.
  • Is my data sent anywhere? No. Your log stays on your iPhone.
