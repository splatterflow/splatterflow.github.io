Privacy Policy

For the Slow Drip iOS app · Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Splatterflow (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the Slow Drip mobile application (the “App”). Slow Drip keeps your caffeine log on your device. It collects no personal information, requires no account, and never sells or shares data.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect personal data (no names, emails, contacts, or location).
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies of any kind.
  • The App does not require an account, login, or registration.
  • Your drinks, your settings and your Pro purchase status are stored locally on your device. We have no server and never receive them.
  • Apple Health is used only if you switch it on, and only for caffeine.

2. What the App stores on your device

Slow Drip keeps the following in its own storage on your device: the drinks you log (what, how many milligrams of caffeine, and when), drinks you add yourself, your bedtime, caffeine sensitivity and daily limit, the language and appearance you chose, whether you have seen the introduction, how many free planner checks you have used, and whether you have purchased Slow Drip Pro. The App’s widgets and Live Activity read the same storage on the device. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the App deletes it. It may be included in your own device backup, which is controlled by you and Apple.

3. Apple Health

Apple Health sync is optional, is part of Slow Drip Pro, and is off until you switch it on and grant permission in the system prompt. When it is on, the App:

  • writes the caffeine of each drink you log to Apple Health, and removes or updates that entry when you delete the drink or change its time;
  • reads caffeine entries from the last few days that other apps saved to Apple Health, so they count in your curve.

The App accesses no other type of health data. Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health; it is never sent to us, never stored in iCloud by the App, and never used for advertising, marketing or data mining. Switching the sync off removes the entries imported from other apps from Slow Drip. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

4. Notifications and Siri

The optional last-call reminder is scheduled on your device; no notification passes through a server of ours. The “Log caffeine” action for Siri and Shortcuts runs on your device and writes only to the App’s own storage. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Siri itself.

5. Network use

The App works fully offline. The only network requests it makes are to fetch a small public list of other Splatterflow apps and their icons, shown in Settings under “More from Splatterflow”, and to talk to Apple’s App Store for the price and your purchase. The list request carries no information about you and is cached on your device. Opening one of those apps shows Apple’s own App Store page inside the App; Apple’s privacy policy applies to that page.

6. Purchases

Slow Drip Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The App only records on your device that the purchase was made. You can restore the purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple Account.

7. Not medical advice

Slow Drip shows an estimate based on average caffeine metabolism. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date.
